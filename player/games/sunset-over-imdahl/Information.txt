If anyone would like to host this game on their own site, go right ahead.
Just don't change any files or change/remove this Information.txt.

If anyone would like to use any of the scripts or graphics from this game,
go right ahead with that as well. I believe that the resources used in game
making should always be open to other creators, so that people can
benefit from each others work. Only that way, the collective graphical
standard can continue to go up. In my opinion, people who go into great
effort to lock up their games are doing a horrible thing.

Some credit is always nice though, but that's up to you.
